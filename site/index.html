  • GOOG (Alphabet) — Alphabet-Blackstone $5B AI Cloud Joint Venture Project Braid Faces Data Center Delays 0.1%

    Alphabet and Blackstone's $5 billion joint venture, Project Braid, designed to lease Google's AI TPUs to enterprise customers starting in 2027, has encountered notable delays across multiple planned data center sites. Google terminated a build near Cheyenne, Wyoming after losing confidence in developer Crusoe's execution capability and is now downsizing and reapplying for permits under its own command. Additional sites fell through due to a severe nationwide shortage of power transformers carrying lead times of almost a year, alongside a freeze on new data center approvals in Texas. Despite the hurdles, the venture's CEO, Benjamin Treynor Sloss, stated that Project Braid remains on track to deliver its target of 500 megawatts next year.
