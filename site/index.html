  • PAGS (PagSeguro Digital Ltd.) — PagSeguro Digital Outlines R$2.0 Billion Dividend Target and Authorizes New US$150 Million Share Buyback 7.7%

    PagSeguro Digital's Board of Directors authorized its fourth share repurchase program to buy back up to US$150 million of outstanding Class A common shares, effective immediately with no fixed expiration date. In addition, the Brazilian financial technology company announced a long-term capital return target to distribute at least R$2.0 billion in dividends across the years 2027 and 2028, translating to R$1.0 billion per year. This aggressive shareholder return initiative drove a 7.66% rally in PAGS shares on the New York Stock Exchange.

  • NVDA (Nvidia) — Nvidia Reaches Definitive Agreement to Acquire AI Platform Hugging Face for $12.93 Billion 0.8%

    Nvidia entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Hugging Face, the leading open-source repository and developer platform for artificial intelligence, in a landmark transaction valued at approximately $12.93 billion (exactly $12,930,300,000). Under the terms of the all-stock transaction, Nvidia will pay an $11.9 billion purchase price to Hugging Face stockholders and establish an equity-based retention program of up to $1.0 billion for employees. The deal, which is expected to close in the first half of 2027 pending regulatory approvals, is designed to embed Nvidia's architecture deep within developer workflows while pledging to keep the platform open, interoperable, and multi-accelerator compatible for the entire AI community.

  • NOW (ServiceNow) — ServiceNow Bolsters Agentic AI and CRM Moat via Sweep M&A and Expanded Experian Alliance -3.0%

    ServiceNow has reportedly acquired Israeli agentic AI startup Sweep in a transaction estimated to be worth hundreds of millions of dollars. Sweep provides a specialized workspace that works across enterprise networks like Salesforce and HubSpot, monitoring metadata to identify bottlenecks and orchestrate workflows. Concurrently, Experian plc announced it is significantly expanding its global partnership with ServiceNow to natively integrate Experian's Ascend analytics platform. The integration is designed to let autonomous AI agents directly access Experian's secure intelligence to automate complex, regulated workflows like fraud verification, credit-rating risk checks, and model governance at scale.

  • BA (The Boeing Company) — Boeing Secures 777-8F Cargo Jet LOI and Faces Unfavorable Contract Liabilities at Spirit AeroSystems 0.8%

    Indian cargo carrier Afcom Holdings signed a Letter of Intent (LOI) to acquire up to four next-generation Boeing 777-8 Freighters (777-8F) to launch widebody intercontinental routes from Chennai. Separately, media reports revealed that Boeing is uncovering hundreds of millions of dollars in additional liabilities from unfavorable contracts at fuselage maker Spirit AeroSystems, which Boeing agreed to acquire for $8.4 billion. These developments occurred as Boeing continues its turnaround efforts ahead of CEO Kelly Ortberg's scheduled appearance at the Morgan Stanley Laguna Conference on September 16.
