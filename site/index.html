  • NVO (Novo Nordisk) — Novo Nordisk Announces Blockbuster Wegovy Pediatric Trial Win and Halts Two Phase 3 Heart Failure Studies -1.9%

    Novo Nordisk announced that the Phase 3 STEP Young trial of once-weekly semaglutide (Wegovy) met its primary endpoint in children aged 6 to under 12 years with obesity, showing that 40.4% of treated children dropped below the obesity threshold after 68 weeks. At the same time, the company experienced a significant pipeline setback by halting two major Phase 3 trials—Hermes and Athena, which enrolled 4,900 and 673 patients respectively—testing ziltivekimab in heart failure patients. The decision followed an independent monitoring committee finding a low likelihood of efficacy, further complicating Novo's efforts to diversify its product line beyond diabetes and obesity treatments.

Following the announcement, the stock dropped 5.13% to close at GBX 18.50.

  • UBER (Uber Technologies, Inc.) — Uber Weighs Debut Euro-Denominated Bond Sale Following Investment-Grade Upgrades

    Uber has hired several major investment banks to hold calls with investors on September 7 and 8, 2026, to evaluate demand for its first-ever Euro-denominated bond offering. The potential transaction is expected to consist of senior unsecured fixed-rate notes split across five tranches with maturities of 3, 6, 8, 12, and 20 years. Uber appointed Goldman Sachs, BNP Paribas, Bank of America Securities, Deutsche Bank, and Morgan Stanley to act as bookrunners. The strategic financing move follows recent upgrades giving Uber investment-grade status from both S&P (BBB+) and Moody's (Baa2).
